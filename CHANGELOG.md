# What's New in Vivid Realms

Written for players, not developers. The launcher reads this file straight from
GitHub, so anything added here shows up for everyone the next time they open it —
no new launcher download needed.

Format matters to the launcher's parser: `## ` starts a dated entry, `### ` starts
a group inside it, and `- ` is a bullet. Newest at the top.

---

## 10 September 2026

### Achievements

- Every achievement now has its own collectible fantasy badge. Display up to
  three earned badges on your Profile, and swap them whenever you like.
- Click a member's name in the campaign hub to see their featured badges.
  Only chosen badges are shared; other achievement progress stays private.
- Seven new account-wide achievements track your first maximum and minimum
  rolls, two of either in a row, ten of either overall, and your first completed
  quest. Find your progress and unlocked titles on **Profile**.
- Maximum means every kept die shows its highest face; minimum means every
  kept die shows 1. Modifiers and discarded dice do not count. Each roll counts
  once, and ordinary rolls break streaks. Manual and blind rolls are excluded.
- Quest completion counts for current campaign members other than spectators,
  including quests with no VCoin reward. Tracking starts with this update.
- Cosmetic rewards are planned for later; earned achievements stay saved.

### Vibrant daylight themes

- Five new bright themes: **Beacon Daybreak**, **Atlas Skyglass**, **Mistral Bloom**,
  **Vacuo Sunburst**, and **Aura Pop**.
- Designed for bright rooms, with solid light panels, clear borders, dark text,
  readable map labels, and no film grain. Primary buttons use white text, and
  keyboard focus gets a clearly visible outline.
- Find them in the theme menu in the top bar for both Remnant and D&D 5e campaigns.
- Clear Daylight, High Contrast Night, and all existing themes are unchanged.

## 9 September 2026

### Easier to read

- Two new themes built specifically for readability: **Clear Daylight**, a bright
  high-contrast look, and **High Contrast Night**, for those who want maximum
  clarity without a bright screen.
- Both remove the film grain and paper texture that sat over the text in the
  other themes, and both give buttons and inputs a much more visible outline.
- Pick either from the theme menu in the top bar. They work in both Remnant and
  D&D 5e campaigns.

### Chat

- Chat now makes a sound when someone speaks, so you stop missing messages while
  you're staring at the battle map.
- Whispers use a different, brighter tone — you can tell someone messaged you
  privately without looking at the screen.
- There's a bell button on the chat tabs to mute it if you'd rather not hear it.
  Your choice is remembered.

### For the GM

- New **View as player** button on the battle map. It shows you the table exactly
  as your players see it — fog at full strength, hidden objects gone, secret NPC
  names withheld, and your GM tools tucked away. A banner keeps you from
  forgetting you're in it.

### Fixed

- Cleaned up garbled icons and punctuation that had crept across the interface,
  including the coin icon on the GM's VCoin panel and the battle map's ruler,
  draw and fog tool icons.

## 23 August 2026

### Accounts

- Added password recovery, so a forgotten password no longer means a new account.

### The Emporium

- The shop has a goblin merchant now. He reacts when you spend.
- Polished the shop layout throughout.

### VCoins

- Completing a quest now pays the whole party in VCoins.
- The GM can hand out VCoins directly for whatever they think deserves it, either
  to one player or to everyone at once.
- GMs get a VCoin Rewards panel on the dashboard showing recent payouts.

### Cosmetics

- Unlockable **turn-start effects** — a flourish around your token when initiative
  reaches you. Aura pulse, Dust ignition, frost ring, voltage surge, shadow bloom
  and a rose entrance.
- Unlockable **natural 20 and natural 1 effects**, so your critical successes and
  spectacular failures look the way you want them to.
- Dice trail cosmetics: ember, frost, shadow, lightning and rose petals.

## 7 August 2026

- Multiplayer dice animations are now synchronized — everyone at the table sees
  the same roll tumble at the same moment, instead of each screen doing its own
  thing.

## 21 July 2026

### For the GM

- First-time GM onboarding tour and a guide library.
- NPCs can be assigned to specific players to control, rather than all-or-nothing.
- Campaign notes, with Google Docs and Drive links.
- Dashboard panel layouts now save to the server, so your setup follows you to
  any computer.

### Combat

- Initiative tie-breakers and a clearer turn order display.

### Remnant

- The Remnant monster library is complete, and Grimm artwork is preserved when
  you place a token on the map.
- Full Remnant character sheet overhaul, with collapsible sections and an
  overview at the top.

## 20 July 2026

- Dust vial rules with charge tracking, and elemental effects on map tokens.
- Sustained Semblance upkeep controls, including the 2 Aura per round cost.
