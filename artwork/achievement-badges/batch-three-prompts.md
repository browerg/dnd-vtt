# Batch three artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Archival PNGs are in this
directory; runtime WebPs are in `client/public/assets/achievements`.

Each subject used this shared prompt:

> Create one square collectible fantasy achievement badge for Vivid Realms.
> Subject: [subject below]. Polished illustrated 3D enamel and metal videogame
> medal, luminous jewel colors and warm gold trim, chunky clear silhouette
> legible at 72 pixels. One centered emblem occupying 80% of image, generous
> even margin. Opaque near-black navy background, subtle halo, no lettering,
> no words, no numbers, no watermark, no border outside emblem. Cohesive
> premium RPG achievement collection.

- **first-badge**: a single ornate golden medal with a ruby star being pinned to a dark velvet ribbon.
- **first-campaign**: a round tavern table with three jewel-colored empty chairs and a glowing central lantern.
- **first-dm**: an open gilded dungeon master screen, a dragon silhouette crest and a floating violet die.
- **first-character**: a brave young adventurer silhouette stepping through a bright teal arched doorway.
- **three-characters**: three distinct heroic masks or helmets in a triangular heraldic grouping, warrior mage ranger.
- **profile-bio**: a silver feather quill writing a glowing golden line in a small open book.
- **profile-palette**: a crystalline prism fanning amber teal rose and violet rays in a golden ring.
- **first-cosmetic**: an elegant enchanted midnight cloak with a brilliant emerald clasp.
- **triple-min**: three cracked dark dice stacked above a tiny indomitable red ember, dramatic funny bad luck.
- **ten-quests**: a golden compass laid over a stack of completed quest scrolls with teal wax seals.
